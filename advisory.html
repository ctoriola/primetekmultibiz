Our approach

Advice that starts from the operator's view

Equipment alone does not create security. Doctrine, intelligence, planning and people determine whether capability turns into outcomes.

PrimeTEK's advisory practice brings together senior military experience, threat intelligence and practical knowledge of the equipment we supply. We help the Armed Forces, paramilitary agencies and critical infrastructure operators understand their threat picture, set priorities and build the capability to respond. We then stay alongside them as the situation evolves.

Services

What we advise on

Counterterrorism strategy

Threat-led strategies for countering insurgency and terrorism: force posture, intelligence integration and phased campaign planning.

Tactical operations support

Planning support, capability integration and operator familiarisation so that new equipment contributes on the ground from day one.

Crisis response

Rapid assessment and response planning when incidents occur, with contingency plans and drills that prepare organisations before they do.

Threat intelligence

Collection, analysis and reporting that turns raw ISR data and open-source information into decisions commanders can act on.

Critical infrastructure protection

Security risk assessments and layered protection plans for energy, oil and gas, transport and government facilities.

Procurement advisory

Independent guidance on requirements, specifications and sourcing, drawing on our network of manufacturers and our own production line.

Engagement model

How an engagement runs

  1. Brief

    A confidential briefing to understand your mandate, environment and constraints.

  2. Assess

    Threat and vulnerability assessment across people, sites, assets and operations.

  3. Plan

    A prioritised plan that combines doctrine, intelligence, training and equipment.

  4. Support

    Implementation support, review and adjustment as the threat picture changes.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you need? Contact our team.

Who can engage PrimeTEK?

We work with government, military, paramilitary and law-enforcement bodies, and with operators of critical infrastructure. Some capabilities are supplied only to authorised government end users.

Do you only advise, or also supply equipment?

Both. Our advisory work is often the starting point for a capability programme that includes ISR, protected mobility, PPE, armouries and locally manufactured equipment, but we also run standalone advisory engagements.

How is confidential information handled?

Engagements are handled on a need-to-know basis, and we sign confidentiality agreements as standard. Sensitive discussions take place in person or over agreed secure channels.

Can you support operator familiarisation?

Yes. We support the handover of equipment with operator familiarisation, maintenance guidance and after-sales support so capability stays mission-ready.

Where are you based?

Our main office is at No 4 Ezugwu Street, Phase 1, ACO Estate, Sabon Lugbe, Airport Road, Abuja, with a branch office at El-panthomath Park, 14B Accra Street, Wuse Zone 5, Abuja FCT.
