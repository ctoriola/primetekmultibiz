About PrimeTEK

Security begins with readiness

An Abuja-based defence and security company founded on Nigerian military command experience, serving the institutions that keep the country safe.

Our story

Threats move first. We move earlier.

Nigeria's security environment does not wait for preparation to catch up. Threats move first, and the organisations that matter are the ones already positioned before they do.

PrimeTEK Multi Biz Solutions operates at that point of readiness. We deliver military-grade security solutions, intelligence-driven risk mitigation and strategic defence consulting to the Armed Forces, paramilitary agencies and operators of critical national infrastructure.

Our work spans counterterrorism strategy, tactical operations support, crisis response and threat intelligence, built on a leadership background drawn directly from Nigerian military command. That foundation shapes how we approach every engagement: from the operator's point of view, with the mission as the measure of success.

Alongside our advisory work, we supply and integrate a portfolio of intelligence, protection and action capabilities, and we manufacture a growing range of tactical hardware, munitions and unmanned systems here in Nigeria.

Purpose

What drives us

Mission

To give Nigeria's security forces and critical infrastructure operators the intelligence, protection and capability to act before threats become crises.

Vision

A Nigeria whose defence and security needs are met increasingly from home: designed for local conditions, built by local hands and supported locally.

Promise

Anticipate. Secure. Dominate. Every product and engagement we deliver serves one of those three outcomes, and together they form a single operational doctrine.

Strategic advantage

How we think about security

01 / ANTICIPATE

See it first

Threats are cheaper to manage before they arrive. Intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance capabilities, from surveillance drones to satellite imagery and early warning systems, give commanders the awareness to act before an incident becomes a crisis.

Anticipate
02 / SECURE

Stand firm

Awareness only matters if it is followed by protection. Counter-UAS systems, protected mobility and ballistic equipment give personnel and infrastructure the physical and electronic defences to withstand the threats that anticipation identifies.

Secure
03 / DOMINATE

Act decisively

Protection creates the space to act decisively. Tactical vehicles, robotics, and weapons and ammunition sourcing convert operational advantage into mission outcomes, on the ground and in the air.

Dominate
Our values

The standards we hold

We work with institutions where the cost of failure is measured in lives. These principles govern how we behave on every engagement.

  • Readiness. We prepare before we are asked, so our clients are positioned before the threat is.
  • Discretion. Client information, deployments and capabilities are handled on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Operational realism. Our advice and equipment are judged by how they perform in the field, not on paper.
  • National capability. Wherever it makes sense, we build, maintain and support in Nigeria.
  • Accountability. We stand behind what we deliver, from sourcing through to after-sales support.
Milestones

A track record rooted in service

Key moments in the journey that shaped PrimeTEK and its leadership.

  1. Senior military command

    Our Executive Chairman serves as Commander of the Infantry Corps of the Nigerian Army and as Director General of the Defence Industries Corporation of Nigeria (DICON).

  2. 2019: Ezugwu MRAP unveiled

    Nigeria's indigenous MRAP, developed with the Command Engineering Depot, is unveiled and inducted into Nigerian Army service.

  3. August 2025: PrimeTEK incorporated

    PrimeTEK Multi-Biz Solutions Ltd is incorporated in Abuja under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 2020 (RC 8708646).

  4. Today: local production line

    PrimeTEK scales domestic manufacture of 16 product lines, from early warning devices and grenades to drones and remote weapon stations.

Registered & accountable

PrimeTEK Multi Biz Solutions Ltd is incorporated with the Corporate Affairs Commission of the Federal Republic of Nigeria as a private company limited by shares. Company registration no. 8708646.
