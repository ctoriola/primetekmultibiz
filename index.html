Featured platform

Ezugwu MRAP

Nigeria's indigenous Mine-Resistant Ambush Protected vehicle, developed under Major General V.O. Ezugwu (Rtd) at the Defence Industries Corporation of Nigeria with the Command Engineering Depot.

Up to 12 crew4x4 chassis360° traversable turretIn service since 2019

The Ezugwu has been inducted into service with the Nigerian Army and has undergone iterative upgrades to meet evolving threats, with additional units ordered for theatres where protection against ambush and explosive threats is critical.

Leadership

Led from military command

Major General V.O. Ezugwu (Rtd), Executive Chairman

A former Commander of the Infantry Corps of the Nigerian Army, with a career spanning counter-insurgency operations, force readiness and the coordination of personnel, weapons and logistics under real combat conditions.

As former Director General of DICON, he led the development of the Ezugwu MRAP, a vehicle now in service with the Nigerian Army. His decades of senior engagement underpin PrimeTEK's credibility and access within the global defence ecosystem.
